About Roomzy

Why we built a simpler way to find housing and roommates in Yangon.

Roomzy started as a school project prototype to solve a real problem: finding an affordable, trustworthy place to live in Yangon shouldn't require endless phone calls, sketchy Facebook groups, or hoping a stranger's roommate ad is legit.

We built Roomzy around three ideas: affordability (listings starting from 300,000 MMK/month), compatibility (matching roommates by lifestyle and budget, not just location), and trust (identity verification so you know who you're dealing with before you meet).

This version of Roomzy is a static frontend prototype — a visual and interactive demo of the concept, built to showcase the product idea before a full backend build.
