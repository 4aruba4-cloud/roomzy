Identity Verification Policy

How Roomzy's verification concept works (prototype description).

Every Roomzy account is designed to go through identity verification so users always know who they're dealing with.

  • Government ID checked by our team
  • Legal information is never shown publicly
  • No need to share your phone number early
  • Admin-reviewed property information

In this prototype, verification status is simulated (Verified / Pending) and not connected to a real ID-check system.
